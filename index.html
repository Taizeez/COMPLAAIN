    Jurisdictional PII & Fintech Coverage

    Complaain embeds rigorous algorithmic validation (Luhn checksums, Mod-97 IBAN checks, ABA routing algorithms) and extensive contextual pattern libraries.

    Nigeria & African Fintech

    Bank Verification Number (BVN): 11-digit unique identifier with context proximity filters.

    NIN: 11-digit National Identity Number.

    NUBAN: 10-digit Nigerian Uniform Bank Account Numbers.

    CAC Registration: Corporate Affairs Commission RC, BN, IT, LLP registrations.

    Local Telecoms: +234, 080, 081, 090, 091, 070 mobile formats.

    UK & European Legal

    Sort Codes: UK 6-digit bank routing + 8-digit account numbers.

    IBAN: International Bank Accounts verified with ISO 7064 Modulo 97-10.

    National Insurance (NI): Official HMRC 9-character format.

    Legal Citations: Neutral citations (UKSC, EWCA Civ/Crim, UKEAT, Employment Tribunals).

    Statutory Citations: Acts of Parliament, UK GDPR, Statutory Instruments.

    US & Global Financial

    Credit / Debit Cards: 13–19 digit PAN validated with Luhn Algorithm to eliminate false positives.

    SSN: US Social Security Numbers with geographic area validation.

    ABA Routing: 9-digit US Federal Reserve transit numbers.

    SWIFT / BIC: 8 or 11 character ISO 9362 bank identifier codes.

    Passports: International biometric passports across jurisdictions.
